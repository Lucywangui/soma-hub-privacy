SOMA HUB Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 October 2026

SOMA HUB provides educational revision materials, topical questions, quizzes and exam practice for learners following the Kenyan curriculum. This policy explains how information may be collected, used, protected and shared when you use the app.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use, SOMA HUB may process:

  • Profile information, such as a student's name, grade or class, school name if provided, and selected avatar.
  • Learning activity, such as quiz responses, scores, progress and completed revision materials.
  • Account information needed to provide and maintain your account.
  • Payment records, transaction references and wallet activity needed to process purchases and prevent duplicate transactions.
  • Technical information necessary to operate, secure and troubleshoot the service.

We do not intend to collect information that is unnecessary for providing the service. The information actually collected depends on the features enabled in the version you use.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to provide learning materials, save progress, calculate scores, manage accounts and purchases, verify transactions, provide support, protect the service against fraud or misuse, and meet legal obligations.

3. Payments and Third-Party Services

When you make a payment, payment information may be processed by third-party payment providers, including IntaSend and the relevant mobile-money provider, as applicable. Those providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies. SOMA HUB does not ask users to provide their mobile-money PIN in the app.

4. Sharing of Information

Information may be shared with service providers where necessary to operate the app, process payments, maintain security or comply with applicable law. We do not sell personal information.

5. Children and Learners

SOMA HUB is designed to support learners. Parents or guardians should assist younger learners with account setup and purchases where appropriate. We aim to collect only information reasonably needed to provide the educational service.

6. Data Security and Retention

We take reasonable measures to protect information from unauthorized access, loss, misuse or disclosure. Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including security, financial recordkeeping and legal requirements.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Your Choices and Data Requests

You may contact us to ask about accessing, correcting or deleting personal information associated with your use of SOMA HUB, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements. If the app supports account creation, users can also use the account-deletion method provided by the app or its designated deletion webpage.

8. Notifications

If notifications are enabled, SOMA HUB may send learning reminders or other app-related notifications. You can manage notification permissions through your device settings.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the app, our practices or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date when appropriate.

10. Contact Us

For privacy questions or requests, contact the SOMA HUB support team at the official support email address provided in the app or on the official SOMA HUB website.
